RESEARCH STATEMENT
My research interrogates the convergence of technology and embodiment through the lens of posthumanism, with a specific focus on cyberpunk literature. Engaging critically with texts such as Altered Carbon and Neuromancer, I examine how digital consciousness, artificial embodiment, and technocapitalism reshape notions of identity, mortality, and agency. Drawing on theorists like Haraway and Hayles, my work situates technological apotheosis within broader biopolitical and philosophical frameworks. The objective is to map the evolving human-machine interface as both a literary and socio-technological construct.
